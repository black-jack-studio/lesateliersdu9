## Scope & mode

Whole site (6 pages + new Casiers locaux page), Persuade mode: a first-time local visitor must understand the place, pick Stéphanie vs. Vincent vs. rooms vs. lockers, and act (call/email/book/HelloAsso).

## Audience / job / constraints

Local residents near Sainte-Catherine-de-Fierbois (37), mostly on mobile, deciding whether to book a workshop, rent a room, or grab local-producer goods. Must preserve: real contact info, real pricing where it exists (events), real address, both practitioners named distinctly. Placeholder pricing (rooms, lockers) must read as swappable, never as confirmed fact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site reads as the client's own printed kit come to life — a fir-green workshop stamp/poster system built around the "9" as a graphic module marker — not the soft, cream, rounded-card wellness-studio template every competitor in this category defaults to.

OWN-WORLD: Fir-green (#1F4A33) fields on beige (#EDE6D2) paper ground; white (#FFFFFF) reversed text on green; near-black (#141414) body copy on beige; taupe (#CFC7B6) hairlines as the only structural device — no shadows, no soft rounded cards. Londrina Solid, uppercase and tight, for all display/headings, echoing the kit's wordmark and sticker treatment; DM Sans for body and UI. Numbered module markers (01, 02, 03...) borrowed from the kit's sticker/stamp motif mark each practitioner/section as its own "atelier". Real on-site photos get a uniform fir-green duotone/wash treatment so the older bohemian-zen photography reads as one system instead of fighting the new identity.

STORY: Land → understand this is one real warm place run by two named people → scan the two practitioner modules and the two standalone offers (rooms, lockers) as equal-weight stamped tiles → pick the one relevant action → act through a single consistent sticky mobile CTA (call / book / HelloAsso link) that travels with the visitor down the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed fir-green band top with beige wordmark lockup upper-left, one duotone hero photo bleeding to the viewport edge beneath it, large stacked Londrina Solid headline in beige/white over the green, one primary CTA pill (beige-on-green) directly under the headline, thin taupe rule closing the band before the page drops to the beige scroll body.

FORM: Custom editorial/stamp system derived directly from the client-supplied brand kit (binding palette, type, logo, stickers) rather than a borrowed template or a generic wellness-studio shell. Direction decided straight from the pinned brand kit per "the brief wins": palette, type, and logo are client-binding facts, not open design choices, so the concept tournament is skipped as disproportionate to a pinned-identity rebuild — the remaining open decision was only composition, resolved here. No image generation is available this session (code-led only; no comp round).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
